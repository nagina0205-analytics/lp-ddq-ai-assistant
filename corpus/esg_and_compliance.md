# Northwind Ventures: ESG, Compliance and Operations

All facts below are made up for this demo.

## Responsible investment

Northwind screens every new investment against an exclusion list that covers weapons manufacturing, tobacco and thermal coal. Portfolio companies with more than 50 employees are asked to complete an annual ESG survey.

## Regulatory status

Northwind is registered with the SEC as an investment adviser. The firm's Form ADV is updated each year and available on request.

## Compliance

The chief compliance officer reports directly to the managing partner. All employees complete annual compliance training and certify the code of ethics. Personal trading requires pre-clearance.

## Cybersecurity

All employees use multi-factor authentication. The firm runs an external penetration test every year and phishing simulations every quarter. LP data is stored in the fund administrator's system and encrypted at rest.

## Business continuity

The business continuity plan is tested once a year. Critical systems can be restored within 24 hours.
