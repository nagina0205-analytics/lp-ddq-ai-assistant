# Northwind Ventures: LP Reporting and Communication

All facts below are made up for this demo.

## Quarterly reports

Limited partners receive a quarterly report within 60 days after each quarter end. It includes capital account statements, portfolio company updates, fund-level performance (IRR, TVPI and DPI) and a schedule of investments.

The fourth-quarter report is delivered with the audited financial statements, within 120 days after year end.

## Capital calls and distributions

Capital call notices are sent at least 10 business days before the funding date. Distribution notices are sent at least 5 business days before payment.

All notices are posted to the LP portal and sent by email to each LP's authorized contacts.

## Annual meeting

Northwind holds an annual LP meeting each spring in San Francisco, with a virtual option. The LP Advisory Committee meets twice a year.

## Data requests

LPs can request additional data through the investor relations team. Standard requests are answered within five business days. Requests for ILPA reporting templates are supported.
