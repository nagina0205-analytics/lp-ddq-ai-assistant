# Northwind Ventures: Valuation Policy

All facts below are made up for this demo.

## Approach

Portfolio companies are held at fair value under ASC 820. Valuations are prepared every quarter by the finance team and reviewed by the valuation committee before LP reports go out.

The primary method is the most recent priced financing round, adjusted for company performance and market conditions since that round. When no recent round exists, the team uses comparable public company multiples.

## Valuation committee

The valuation committee has three members: the chief financial officer, one general partner who does not sit on the company's board, and an independent advisor. The committee meets within 30 days after each quarter end.

## Audit

The annual audit is performed by an independent accounting firm. Audited financial statements are delivered to limited partners within 120 days after year end.

## Write-downs

A company is written down when it misses its plan by more than 40% for two straight quarters, or when it raises money at a lower price than its last round.
