# Northwind Ventures Fund III: Fund Overview

Northwind Ventures is a fictional venture capital firm used for this demo. All facts below are made up.

## Fund terms

Fund III closed in March 2024 with total commitments of $600 million from 41 limited partners. The general partner commitment is 2% of total commitments, or $12 million.

The investment period is five years from the final close. The fund term is ten years, with two optional one-year extensions that require approval from the LP Advisory Committee.

## Strategy

Fund III invests in seed and Series A companies in enterprise software, fintech and AI infrastructure. The target portfolio is 30 to 35 companies. Initial checks range from $3 million to $15 million.

About 50% of the fund is reserved for follow-on investments in existing portfolio companies.

## Team

The investment team has six general partners and eleven investment professionals. The chief financial officer oversees fund accounting, LP reporting and the relationship with the fund administrator.

## Key person

The key person provision covers the two founding general partners. If either of them stops devoting substantially all of their business time to the fund, the investment period is suspended until the LP Advisory Committee votes on how to proceed.
