# Northwind Ventures Fund III: Fees and Expenses

All facts below are made up for this demo.

## Management fee

The management fee is 2.5% per year on committed capital during the investment period. After the investment period ends, the fee steps down to 1.5% per year on invested capital that has not been realized or written off.

Management fees are charged quarterly in advance.

## Carried interest

The general partner receives 20% carried interest on fund profits. Carry rises to 25% once the fund has returned three times paid-in capital to limited partners.

Carry is calculated on a whole-fund basis. There is no preferred return hurdle. A full general partner clawback applies at the end of the fund term.

## Fund expenses

The fund pays legal, audit, fund administration and tax preparation costs. Organizational expenses are capped at $1.5 million. Any amount above the cap reduces the management fee.

Transaction fees and board fees received from portfolio companies offset the management fee at 100%.
